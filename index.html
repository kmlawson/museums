Museums

Study materials drawn from 77 books and articles on museums, their history and their theory.

A history of the museumAn illustrated, footnoted history in 66 stops, from the Mouseion of Alexandria to restitution today. Walk it along twenty pathways: chronological, thematic (power, empire, East and Southeast Asia, publics, war and memory), or through ten theoretical concepts taught by example. Most stops are illustrated and link to period sources on the Internet Archive. Contents of the textsThe table of contents of every book, with an overview of each chapter, a short overview of the whole work, and a note on how far East and Southeast Asia are discussed in it. ApproachesThe theoretical inspirations and concepts the texts draw on, and how each work uses them, grouped into eleven families, with each text's framing, methods and the gaps it names. BibliographyEvery work the texts cite, merged and deduplicated, in three sections: secondary literature, primary sources, and theoretical frameworks. Searchable and sortable. Bibliography as a spreadsheetThe same list as a CSV file.
